# Third-party notices

The production dependency tree (`npm ls --omit=dev --all`) is empty: every package is a build dependency.
The build bundles `pg` and its dependencies into `dist/server/main.js`, and Lucide icons into `dist/client`; the font files ship from `client/fonts/`; their notices are below.
