## Icons

Interface icons drawn in code. Some follow the shapes of Lucide icons (https://lucide.dev, ISC licence), redrawn as inline SVG paths: check, chevron, x, plus, minus, arrow-right, circle-alert, triangle-alert, info, eye, inbox, log-out, menu, user, lock, search, star, undo, redo, copy, trash, pencil, download, upload, history, message-square.

The Sprigmap logo mark, the topic icon set (24 icons), the layout and theme tile drawings, the hero ribbons, avatars and arrow are original artwork made for this app.
