Developer interface

Sign in

The read interface over tables and rows is served on this origin under /api. Sign in at POST /api/auth/login and send the token as Authorization: Bearer, or rely on the session cookie. A caller without a role on the owning department receives a 403 refusal; a caller without a session receives 401.

AddressRole neededAnswerWithout a role
GET /api/tablesany roleA top-level array of the tables the caller may read, in name order.Tables of other departments are absent.
GET /api/tables/{slug}viewerOne table with its columns, tier, row allowance, count, content size and log high ordinal.403 with a reason and the field department.
GET /api/tables/{slug}/rowsviewerA top-level array of rows (page, per_page up to 500, view, cursor); the total is in X-Total-Count.403, and no total.
GET /api/tables/{slug}/rows/{id}viewerOne row with its values and version.403
GET /api/tables/{slug}/viewsviewerThe shared views and the caller's private views.403
GET /api/tables/{slug}/logviewerThe change log, newest first, twenty entries a page.403
GET /api/snapshotsany roleThe retained snapshots of the caller's departments.Other departments' snapshots are absent.
GET /api/tiersnoneThe four tiers in grant order with every allowance.Open address.

The refusal body is {"reason": "You hold no role in the claims department", "field": "department"}. Amendments (create, update, delete) need the editor role; locking and publishing need the steward role.
