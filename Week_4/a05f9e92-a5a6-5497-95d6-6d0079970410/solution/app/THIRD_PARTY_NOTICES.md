## Fonts

Both fonts are bundled as self-hosted variable woff2 files under `public/fonts/` and served from the app's own origin,
licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (full text next to each file).

- **Unbounded** (static 800 and 900, latin subset): Copyright 2022 The Unbounded Project Authors (https://github.com/googlefonts/unbounded). Licence text: `public/fonts/OFL-Unbounded.txt`.
- **Onest** (latin subset): Copyright 2021 The Onest Project Authors (https://github.com/simpals/onest). Licence text: `public/fonts/OFL-Onest.txt`.
