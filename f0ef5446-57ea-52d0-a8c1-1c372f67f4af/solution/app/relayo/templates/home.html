Walk in prepared

Relayo reads the invite, the last conversation and the open questions, then hands you a one-page brief five minutes before the call.

Stay in the conversation

While you talk, Relayo takes the notes: decisions, owners and dates appear in a live draft, each one linked to the moment it was said.

Leave with the next steps

When the call ends the summary is ready to send, follow-ups land with their owners and your tools are already up to date.
