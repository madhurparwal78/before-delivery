- Inspector links refresh: while Sanitation is open for the operator the table re-reads `GET /api/inspector-links` every 5 s (the brief asks at least every 10 s) and redraws Status, Last opened, Views and Downloads in place, without moving focus, changing typed "Inspector email" or "Link expires" text or clearing a pending Revoke or Regenerate; a read sent before a link action's result arrived is discarded, so a revoked link never shows Active again; Status is only the server's `status`. (brief: Interactions › Refresh)
- Inspector link: token 43 URL-safe characters, URL `<request origin>/inspect/<token>`; 410 `link_expired` from expiry, 410 `link_revoked` on revoke or for a regenerated link's old token; every 200 adds one access record (`view`, `document`+id, `report`+month). ### 9. Sign-in on the shared tablet (DP-72)
- Failures (401 `invalid_credentials`) are counted per trimmed, lower-cased email in PostgreSQL, known or not. With 5 inside `LINEBOARD_SIGNIN_WINDOW_SECONDS` (900), the next attempt answers 429 `rate_limited` "Too many sign-in attempts. Try again in <n> minutes." with `Retry-After` = whole seconds until the oldest counted failure leaves the window, without checking the password. Attempts for one email are settled one at a time under an advisory lock; a success clears the count. decided: each failure stores its instant (`signin_failures.at`), and the window is applied when the attempt is judged (failures newer than now − the window count; `Retry-After` = seconds until the oldest of them is a full window old), so the configured window always governs the count. The Sign in screen shows the copy, clears the password and focuses Email. (brief: Sign-in throttling, Interactions)
- Sessions: a 256-bit random id (43 URL-safe characters) in `lineboard_session`, stored hashed in PostgreSQL; a successful sign-in ends the session whose cookie came with it; `DELETE /api/session` ends that session for every copy of the cookie; Sign out returns to plain `/`. (brief: API › Sessions, Interactions)
- Shared-tablet hand-over: every `/api` response (success or error) and every screen document (`/`, each screen address, `/inspect/*`) carries `Cache-Control: no-store`; `/inspect/*` and `/api/inspect/*` also carry `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`. Every request reads its person and kitchen from its own session row; nothing about a caller is kept between requests. Sign-out and any 401 on a signed-in screen end the browser's session: requests still on their way are aborted and any answer that arrives is never drawn, even after the next person signs in in the same tab; the hour bank, alert and announcements are cleared and every screen's state goes with it; Back after sign-out shows the sign-in screen or the signed-in person's own view; a page restored from the back-forward cache reloads. (brief: Server boundary, Interactions › Handing over a shared device)
- Return addresses: a signed-out load of a published signed-in screen (`/board`, `/bookings`, `/documents`, `/invoices`, `/reports`, `/sanitation`, `/settings`), or a 401 on one, replaces the history entry with `/?next=<percent-encoded path and query>`; a refused sign-in keeps `next`. After sign-in, or when a signed-in person opens the sign-in address, they land on `next` when valid, otherwise on `/board` with no alert. `next` is decoded once and is valid only when its path is exactly one of those screens, optionally followed by a query, with no backslash, control character, scheme, host, userinfo, fragment or leading `//`. A signed-in person opening a screen outside their role's navigation, directly or through `next`, lands on `/board` by a replaced history entry with the `alert` "That screen is not available for your role." (brief: Business rules › Return addresses, Interactions, Feedback states)
- Deadlocks and serialisation failures roll the transaction back and run it again inside the request. (brief: Hour ledger › Several months)

## Outages (DP-50..DP-53)
- Kill Bill unreachable: confirm answers 202 `pending_billing`, items stay in the outbox; month close 503; emails still sent. Storage unreachable: uploads/downloads 503 with nothing saved; sanitation PDFs 202 `queued`. Banner copy from Feedback states with "<n> billing items waiting · <n> storage items waiting"; after each of the person's own state-changing requests answers, the banner re-reads `GET /api/service-status` at once (besides its 5 s poll; the brief asks at least every 10 s), and every poll and re-read changes only the text inside the same region, which is never removed and re-inserted while a service stays down, so its slide-in never replays and focus never moves. Nothing Ada sees names a server, a port or an error from the billing system. Monitor probes every 5 s; queued work retried against the same configured address. (brief: Services)
- decided: Kill Bill and storage are called over `node:http`, not `fetch`, because `fetch` refuses some ports outright (port 9 among them), and the brief requires retrying "that same configured address every time" until it answers.
- decided: every outbound call opens a fresh connection (no pooled keep-alive sockets), and the status probes count a service as up only when it answers as itself (an authenticated Kill Bill `GET /1.0/kb/nodesInfo` 200; a signed bucket HEAD 200); a 401/403, or an error answer that is not Kill Bill's JSON error body, counts as unavailable, because a socket or answer from whatever the address meant earlier must never stand in for "that same configured address" (brief: Recovery). Kill Bill's `/1.0/healthcheck` is not the probe: it also fails while its internal queues are growing, when Kill Bill is still answering, and the brief refuses a close only "while Kill Bill is unreachable" (brief: Invoices and month close).
- decided: Kill Bill amounts are read back from the decimal text of its JSON straight into integer cents (never through a float), and sent as two-decimal dollar text formatted from cents (brief: Engineering standards "Money is handled as integer cents from end to end").
- decided: when the storage probe reaches a store that accepts the signed request but has no configured bucket (HEAD 404), Lineboard creates that bucket and counts storage as up, so an instance whose bucket could not be provisioned while its address was unreachable still recovers on its own (brief: Recovery "recovers without a restart or a configuration change"; the brief says Lineboard need not create it, not that it must not).
- decided: a queued email the mail server refuses is retried with a growing backoff (at most 60 s apart) while later emails are sent meanwhile, so one bad message never blocks the queue.
- Every instance delivers and counts the whole queue of its own database, under one PostgreSQL advisory lock per database so each item is delivered once; each instance has its own database and bucket. (brief: Recovery "The harness creates each instance's own database and bucket … so no two instances share records"; Queued counts "On a fresh outage instance, both counts are 0")

- Every saved business name is sent to the member's Kill Bill account name through the outbox, delivered in the order saved so the account ends with the last saved name; each character above U+FFFF (an emoji) is sent as U+FFFD and every other character unchanged, because Kill Bill stores only the Basic Multilingual Plane; retried like other outbox work and complete within 30 seconds of billing being reachable; never counted in the queued counts. (brief: Services › Kill Bill, Queued counts)
- decided: each save writes its name to the outbox in the same transaction as the business name; a member's queued names are sent one by one in save order on each delivery pass (at least every 10 s), and an account created after a save is first named with the current business name, then receives the queued names in order (brief: "Every saved business name is sent … through the outbox", "delivered in the order they were saved").

## Server boundary (DP-55, DP-94, DP-96)
- Cross-site requests: every method other than GET and HEAD on any path under `/api`, published or not (`POST /api/session` and `DELETE /api/session` included), is cross-site when `Origin` is present and differs from `http://` + the request's `Host` (compared exactly, port included; `Origin: null` differs), or `Origin` is absent and `Sec-Fetch-Site` is `cross-site` or `same-site`, or both are absent and `Referer` names another origin. It answers `403 {"error":"cross_origin","message":"Requests from other sites are refused."}` as precedence step 1, before any body read, session or key, so it is never counted as a sign-in failure, never answers 429, never ends or creates a session, never stores or consumes an Idempotency-Key, never adds an access record and never answers 404 on an unlisted path. A request with none of the three headers, or with no `Origin` and `Sec-Fetch-Site` `same-origin` or `none`, is allowed. No response carries an `Access-Control-Allow-*` header. The session cookie is HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax. (brief: Server boundary › Cross-site requests, API › Sessions)
- Content type: a request with a body to a JSON endpoint must carry `Content-Type: application/json` (charset allowed); any other type answers 415 `unsupported_media_type` even when the body parses as JSON, and a sign-in so refused is not counted. An endpoint whose request is `{}` also accepts an empty body with or without a content type. (brief: API › Basics)
- Opaque errors: every error body is `{"error","message"}` plus only the extra keys API lists for that code, and `message` is exactly the published copy for its code (Feedback states › Errors) with placeholders filled in; the fixed copies live in one shared table read by the server's error shape and by the screens. An unexpected failure answers `500 {"error":"internal","message":"Lineboard hit an unexpected problem. Try again."}` and its cause goes only to the server log; no handler builds a message from a caught error's text. `GET /api/service-status` carries exactly `billing`, `storage` and `queued{billing,storage}`. (brief: API › Error shape, Server boundary › Opaque errors)
- The `alert` shows only published copy, never a server's raw text: a response that is not Lineboard's JSON shows the internal copy (decided: the table has no other copy for it). On a 422 the field errors show under their fields; the `alert` shows the first field error's message where Interactions says so (Confirm, Block details actions, a move) and otherwise the validation_failed copy "Check the highlighted fields." (brief: Feedback states › How feedback appears, Errors)

## Decided details
- `POST /api/documents` reads a form body: `multipart/form-data` (the published request), or an `application/x-www-form-urlencoded` form, read as the same parts; a url-encoded form cannot carry a file, so like a multipart body with no file part it answers 422 `file` "Choose a file to upload." Any other content type is a wrong content type: 415 `unsupported_media_type` at step 3 of the precedence rule. (brief: API › Endpoints, Field errors "file missing"; decided: the 415 rule names JSON endpoints, and a form with no file is a missing file)
- Every screen document (never `/inspect/*`) carries the signed-in context its cookie opens, exactly the bodies of `GET /api/session` and `GET /api/kitchen` (or null when signed out), in an inline JSON element the browser reads once and removes, so the frame and each screen's tables draw on load with no round trip; the document stays `no-store`, so Back after sign-out re-reads it signed out. (decided: brief Browser support "the board is usable within three seconds of navigation"; Server boundary "Every request reads its person and kitchen from its own session row")
- The board's scrolling frame (`region "Line board"`) takes focus only from code (`tabindex="-1"`, where focus lands when a block leaves the board) and is never a Tab stop; the Tab order is the brief's list. (brief: Interactions › Tab order on the board)
- Mail committed to the outbox wakes the delivery worker at once through PostgreSQL `NOTIFY` (sent on commit only), with a 2 s sweep behind it, so each email follows its action promptly (decided: brief Services › Commit rules, delivery retried until it succeeds).
- Email fields (sign-in, inspector link): valid when the trimmed text is a local part without "@" (RFC 5322 lets a quoted local part hold spaces) then "@" and a dotted domain without spaces; otherwise 422 "Enter a valid email address.". A local part that is not a plain dot-atom is quoted in the SMTP envelope and the To header. (brief: Field errors names the message but not the rule; decided: sign-in failures are counted "whether or not any account has that email", so the form refuses only what cannot be an address)
- Add peak window adds Mon–Fri 16:00–19:00, a complete window the operator edits, so a save never carries an empty row. (decided: the brief is silent on the new row)
- The `hours` message is "That needs <hours> hours; you have <hours> left for <Month YYYY>." exactly as the Field errors table publishes it, ending in a period. (brief: Feedback states › Field errors "not enough hours (field `hours`) | That needs <hours> hours; you have <hours> left for <Month YYYY>."; the Error shape rule makes that table the exact copy; the Hour ledger and Weekly series examples only quote the message inside running prose.)
