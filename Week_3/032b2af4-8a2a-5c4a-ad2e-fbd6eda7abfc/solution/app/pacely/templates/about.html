We believe if you sweat, you're an athlete. Whether you run a first 5K, ride a gran fondo or walk the dog before work, Pacely turns your effort into numbers you can trust, a community that cheers you on and goals that keep you coming back.

Our Founders

Pacely began when a group of weekend runners got tired of arguing about whose watch was right. They built a place where the server does the maths from every track point, so two athletes who run the same stretch always agree on the result.

We're a Global Company

Pacely Labs is a distributed team with athletes on every continent. We work in the time zones we train in, and our calendars, from weekly stats to monthly challenges, do the same.

Privacy and terms

Pacely keeps only the essential session and no analytics identifier, and it never sells or shares your personal information. Choose who sees each activity, make your account private and hide the area around your home with a privacy zone.

Pacely takes no real card payment: choosing a plan starts a 30-day trial. By using Pacely you agree to be kind in comments and to upload only your own recordings.
