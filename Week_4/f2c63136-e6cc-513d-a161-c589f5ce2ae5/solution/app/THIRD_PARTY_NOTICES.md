## Fonts

All three are variable woff2 files (latin subset) taken from the Fontsource packages `@fontsource-variable/inter`, `@fontsource-variable/inter-tight` and `@fontsource-variable/martian-mono` (5.3.0) and bundled in `public/fonts/`. Licence: OFL-1.1 (SIL Open Font License 1.1). The licence texts are in `public/fonts/licences/`.

| Font | File | Licence | Copyright |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inter | inter-latin-wght-normal.woff2 | OFL-1.1 | Copyright 2016 The Inter Project Authors (https://github.com/rsms/inter) |
| Inter Tight | inter-tight-latin-wght-normal.woff2 | OFL-1.1 | Copyright 2022 The Inter Project Authors (https://github.com/rsms/inter-tight) |
| Martian Mono | martian-mono-latin-wght-normal.woff2 | OFL-1.1 | Copyright 2020 The Martian Mono Project Authors (https://github.com/evilmartians/mono) |

## Media

No image, audio, video or model files are shipped. Icons, artwork, the neon wireframe and the wordmark are drawn in code (`original`).
